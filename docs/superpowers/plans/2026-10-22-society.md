# Society continuation implementation plan

> Execution: implement in this session, as authorized by the user's request to design, implement, verify and finish in one commit.

**Goal:** Continue existing college/employment admissions outcomes through an optional society hub to a chosen political-entry boundary.
**Architecture:** One life operator and one major-choice presenter reuse the current Timeline/Effects/Director. Frozen dynamic choices feed common atomic settlement. Calendar months belong to this operator and never reinterpret student turns.
**Tech stack:** Existing static JavaScript, HTML, CSS, localStorage, Node tests, Chromium.
**Spec:** User's adult-life direction and docs/developing/SOCIETY.md.

## Constraints
- Preserve student content, N-year retries, existing IDs, saves, layouts, preview-only school/party dialogs and 25/5 mental model.
- No new stat, server, dependency, replacement engine or actual election/campaign simulation.
- Korean counting age remains visible; legal eligibility uses a conservative age bound because no birth date exists.
- Completed military periods and causes, education and employment changes remain in history.
- One final commit after actual route/save/UI verification.

## Review focus
- Existing waiting admission choice must settle unchanged before the adult bridge.
- Pending adult choices and failed persistence must not charge twice or reroll.
- Illness five ends life even after multiple admission attempts; retry age boundary stops applying once adult.
- Military academic leave preserves credits, school identity and real month duration.
- Active military and public employment cannot use political entry; returning to society keeps prior records.
